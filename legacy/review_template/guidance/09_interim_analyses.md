# Instructions: Interim Analyses:

Original source: `legacy/tex/content/9_interim_analyses.tex`.

If absence of interim analyses is established, replace all bullets with None and record the supporting evidence. Failure to locate an interim section is not evidence of absence. Distinguish efficacy, futility, safety, and sample-size-reestimation looks.

## INTERIM.ADAPTIVE — Adaptive features

Describe group sequential features, blinded/unblinded sample-size re-estimation, arm dropping, hybrid designs, or other adaptations.

## INTERIM.LOOKS — Number of interim looks

State the number and purpose of looks, distinguishing scheduled from other monitoring.

## INTERIM.METHOD — Statistical method/approach

Describe the sponsor method, e.g. O’Brien–Fleming or conditional power. Preserve any cited method references as supplied; do not fabricate references.

## INTERIM.TIMING — Timing and boundaries at each look

Give information fractions/event counts, efficacy/futility/safety/SSR boundaries, alpha spending, and whether futility is nonbinding, where stated. Flag adequacy of safety sample size if early efficacy stopping is relevant as a reviewer assessment.

## INTERIM.OPERATING — Operating characteristics

If available, summarize stopping/continuation probabilities under stated null and alternative scenarios; do not compute or invent missing values.
