# Potential-issue review prompt

Using the supplied draft, evidence log, and documents, suggest potential statistical concerns for reviewer consideration. Consider alignment of objectives/endpoints/hypotheses/analyses, analysis-set membership and treatment assignment, missing data, multiplicity including interim looks, stopping rules, and sizing assumptions. These are topics to examine, not findings to assert automatically.

For each candidate, follow the issue-log template: name the relevant fields/endpoint, evidence IDs, exact stated plan, gap or conflict, proposed actionable sponsor request, and any separate internal explanation. Mark every new candidate unapproved. State when limited access prevents a conclusion. Do not declare a method unacceptable solely because information was not located. Do not claim independent calculations occurred.

After I supply decisions, update issue dispositions and draft sections 12/13 using only approved wording/concerns. Do not duplicate sponsor requests in the internal section. Use None only if I confirm no comments. Then draft section 1 from the approved report and issues, with a separate evidence mapping.
