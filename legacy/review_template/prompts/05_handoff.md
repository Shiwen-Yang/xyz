# Handoff / resume prompt

Prepare a concise handoff for a fresh chat. Include the source register with versions and applicability decisions; sections/fields completed and reviewed; approved concerns and exact wording; unresolved fields and source conflicts; evidence IDs; any independent verification actually supplied; and the next extraction/review task. List documents that must be reattached.

Do not compress away endpoint distinctions, numerical assumptions, or uncertainty. Do not claim a new chat can access old attachments. In the fresh chat, supply the general instructions, handoff, relevant guidance, approved drafts, evidence/issue logs, and required documents before continuing.
